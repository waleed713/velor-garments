Fabric guide

Lawn

Light, breathable cotton for warm days. Wash cold, dry in the shade and press on the reverse to keep prints bright.

Silk and crepe

A soft sheen with a clean drape for formal days. Dry clean, or hand wash cold with a gentle detergent.

Embroidery and organza

Hand finished details for celebrations. Store folded in muslin and steam gently, never press on the embroidery.
